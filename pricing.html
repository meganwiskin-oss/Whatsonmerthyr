Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in updates?

Updates to opening times, photos, descriptions, contact details, social media links and current offers are all included in the monthly price. No extra charges.

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your package at any time. Just get in touch with us to make changes.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contracts required. You can cancel at any time, though we'd love to keep you on board!

How quickly can I get started?

Once you pay the setup fee and provide your information, we'll get your listing live within a few days.

What if I need help with my listing?

We're here to help! Contact us anytime if you need assistance updating your information or optimising your listing.

How do I get listed for free?

If you're a charity, community group, public attraction or park, just contact us with your details and we'll get you added for free.
