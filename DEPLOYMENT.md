## 🚀 Enable GitHub Pages

To make your website live and accessible online, follow these steps:

1. Go to your repository: https://github.com/meganwiskin-oss/Whatsonmerthyr
2. Click on **Settings** (top right)
3. In the left sidebar, click on **Pages**
4. Under "Build and deployment":
   - Source: Select **Deploy from a branch**
   - Branch: Select **main** and **/ (root)**
5. Click **Save**

GitHub will automatically build and deploy your website. It should be live within 1-2 minutes at:
**https://meganwiskin-oss.github.io/Whatsonmerthyr/**
