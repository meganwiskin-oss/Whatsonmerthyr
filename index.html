Walks & Wild Places

Free walks, trails and wild spaces around Merthyr Tydfil — from reservoir loops to woodland paths and heritage trails.

⚠️ Water & Heritage Safety

Water: Rivers, reservoirs and gorges may be cold, deep, fast or slippery. Do not encourage swimming unless specifically authorised and safe.

Heritage: Do not climb ruins, viaducts, quarry faces or unstable masonry.

Nature: Respect SSSI/protected habitats, wildlife, livestock and private land.

Emergency: Call 999 for immediate danger.

Merthyr History

Merthyr Tydfil grew from a small settlement into one of the world's major industrial towns during the Industrial Revolution. Ironworks such as Cyfarthfa, Dowlais, Penydarren and Plymouth drove rapid population growth and technological change.

🍽️ Merthyr Cynon Foodbank

Voucher-based emergency food. Contact the foodbank or a referral agency (Citizens Advice, housing support, health visitors, social services, local charities). A voucher is exchanged for a minimum three-day emergency food parcel.

Phone: 07427 537437

Email: info@merthyrcynon.foodbank.org.uk

Address: Gurnos Christian Centre, 26 Spruce Tree Grove, Merthyr Tydfil CF47 9AU

Helpline: Help through Hardship 0808 208 2138 (Mon–Fri 9am–5pm)

Caves — Specialist Caving Only

⚠️ SPECIALIST CAVING — NOT A CASUAL WALK-IN ATTRACTION

These are specialist cave sites recorded in the Cambrian Cave Registry. They are not tourist attractions. Access requires appropriate equipment, competence and current access permission. Never explore alone. Always check weather, flooding and access before visiting.
